# AgentTap Privacy Policy

AgentTap collects nothing.

- **No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.** The app has no sign-up, no
  identifiers, no third-party SDKs, and makes no connection to any server
  operated by the developer.
- **All traffic stays on your local network.** AgentTap talks only to a
  VibePulse tokenserver that you run on your own computer, at an address
  you choose. The data shown (usage percentages, agent activity, prompts
  you chose to expose) never leaves your network and is never stored by
  the app beyond its current display and a small on-device cache for the
  watch-face complication.
- **The pairing key** used to authorize answers is stored only in your
  watch's keychain and your own computer. It is never transmitted anywhere
  except directly between those two devices on your network, once, during
  pairing you initiate.
- **Demo mode** uses built-in sample data and makes no network requests.

Because no data is collected, there is nothing to sell, share, or delete.
Questions: open an issue on the project repository.
